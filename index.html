Bio

Hi! I am an incoming PhD student at the University of Michigan. Previously, I was a Research Fellow (Pre-Doctoral) at Microsoft Research India, where I was part of the program synthesis group (PROSE) and the AI4Code team in Bengaluru. I worked on AI for Code and AI for Math, with the goal of making automated programming more trustworthy by developing AI systems that generate robust, correct, and verifiable code—leveraging techniques from Formal Methods, Software Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence to ensure the reliability and correctness of automatically generated programs.

I was privileged to work with Aditya Kanade, Ashish Tiwari, Gustavo Soares, Arjun Radhakrishna, Sumit Gulwani, and Arun Iyer.

For fun, I enjoy playing tennis, running and occasional bouldering.
